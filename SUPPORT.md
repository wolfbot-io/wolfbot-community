# Support

## Official Support Channels

WolfBot Community is a free, self-hosted product. Support is provided through the community:

1. **Documentation** — [community.wolfbot.io/docs](https://community.wolfbot.io/docs)
2. **GitHub Discussions** — [Q&A, help, ideas](https://github.com/wolfbot-io/wolfbot-community/discussions)
3. **GitHub Issues** — [Bug reports](https://github.com/wolfbot-io/wolfbot-community/issues)

## Before Asking for Help

1. Search [existing Discussions](https://github.com/wolfbot-io/wolfbot-community/discussions) for your question
2. Check the [Troubleshooting Guide](https://community.wolfbot.io/docs/troubleshooting)
3. Read the relevant [Documentation](https://community.wolfbot.io/docs) page

## Reporting Issues

When reporting a problem, please include:

- **WolfBot version** (found in Settings → About)
- **Operating system** (Windows 10/11, Ubuntu 22.04/24.04, etc.)
- **Installation type** (Setup.exe, .deb)
- **Broker** you're trying to connect (if applicable)
- **Steps to reproduce** the issue
- **Error message or screenshot** (redact any personal/API information)
- **Diagnostics ID** if available from the error dialog

> ⚠️ **Never share your API keys, secret keys, or passwords** in public discussions or issues.
