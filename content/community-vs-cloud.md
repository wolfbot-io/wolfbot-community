## WolfBot Community

### Best for:
- Traders who want full control of their infrastructure
- Technical users comfortable with self-hosting
- Those who want to test WolfBot for free — one platform for crypto and MT5
- Running on a personal PC or VPS
- Budget-conscious traders

### You are responsible for:
- Installing the software
- Keeping your machine running
- Managing backups
- Network/firewall configuration
- Applying updates (one-click but manual)

### Key benefits:
- ✅ Completely free
- ✅ Your data stays on your machine
- ✅ No subscription commitment
- ✅ Same trading engine as Cloud — crypto + futures + MT5
- ✅ Full API access
- ✅ Open development via GitHub

- ✅ Direct support from the team

## Common Transition Paths

### Start Community → Go Cloud

Many users start with Community to:
1. Test WolfBot risk-free (free, simulation)
2. Validate strategies across crypto and MT5 markets
3. Get comfortable with the unified interface
4. Move to Cloud when ready for managed 24/7 trading

### Cloud → Community

Cloud users sometimes use Community for:
- Development and testing
- Running custom strategies locally
- Backup trading environment
- Privacy-sensitive operations

## Can I Use Both?

Yes! You can run both:

- **Community** on your PC for testing/development
- **Cloud** for production 24/7 trading

They are separate installations and don't interfere with each other.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Community a "limited" version of Cloud?**
No. Community has the same trading engine, strategies, and broker support. The same unified platform — crypto, futures, and MT5. The difference is hosting and support.

**Q: Will Community always be free?**
Yes. WolfBot Community is our commitment to providing a free, self-hosted unified trading platform for everyone.

**Q: Can I migrate from Community to Cloud?**
Yes. Export your configuration and strategies, and our team helps with the transition.

**Q: Does Cloud have features Community doesn't?**
Cloud includes managed hosting, team features, advanced analytics, and direct support. The core trading engine — including all market support — is identical.
