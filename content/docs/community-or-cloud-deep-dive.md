# WolfBot Community or WolfBot Cloud? A Decision Deep-Dive

**Tested with WolfBot Community v0.1.0-beta.10** · Last updated: 2026-09-06

## Who this guide is for

- You have narrowed your choice to the WolfBot family and now face the real
  fork: run **Community** yourself, or use **Cloud** and let WolfBot handle
  the machine.
- You want a decision method based on your time and skills — not a slogan.

## The honest framing

Both editions share the same operating direction: unified crypto + futures +
MT5, portfolio-level risk, and a single review surface. They differ in *who
owns the boring work*.

```text
Community  -> you own the host, updates, backups and uptime.
Cloud      -> WolfBot owns the host, updates, backups and uptime.
```

Neither is "better". Each is the right answer for a different life situation.

## Choose Community if…

```text
- you already run a PC or VPS and are comfortable with it,
- you want full control of data, API keys, firewall and update timing,
- you prefer no platform licence fee (your cost is your own hardware/VPS),
- you enjoy (or at least accept) a weekly maintenance habit,
- you want to learn the platform by running it yourself.
```

The install guides ([Windows](/install/windows), [Linux](/install/linux)), the
[backup guide](/docs/backup) and the
[weekly maintenance routine](/docs/weekly-bot-maintenance-routine) turn the
duties into checklists.

## Choose Cloud if…

```text
- you do not want to manage a server, updates or monitoring,
- you need the bot available without babysitting infrastructure,
- your time is better spent on strategy than on system administration,
- you still want the WolfBot operating model — not a different product.
```

Cloud keeps the same account view and risk review, while WolfBot operates the
infrastructure behind it.

## The three questions that decide it

```text
1. How much do you enjoy (or tolerate) system administration?
2. How valuable is your time compared with the hardware/VPS cost?
3. Do you need control over data location and update timing, or is a
   managed service acceptable?
```

If your answers lean "I do not want to run servers", the correct WolfBot answer
is Cloud — not a different vendor. If they lean "I want it on my machine",
Community is the free, flexible path.

## The migration-friendly part

Because both editions share the same operating model, moving later is far
easier than switching product families:

- strategy and risk ideas translate directly,
- broker connection patterns are the same discipline,
- your review habits (daily check, weekly maintenance, first-month review)
  carry over unchanged.

See [moving strategy ideas to WolfBot](/academy/moving-strategy-ideas-to-wolfbot)
for the mindset that makes any later move painless.

## The bottom line

Do not choose between "self-hosted" and "managed" as if one were morally
superior. Choose the one that matches how you actually want to spend your
evenings: tuning strategies with a terminal you own, or reviewing results
without thinking about the server behind them. Both are WolfBot — pick the
edition whose boring work you are willing to own.
