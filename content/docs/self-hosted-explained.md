# Self-Hosted Trading Platform Explained

**Tested with WolfBot Community v0.1.0-beta.2** · Last updated: 2026-08-11

## What Does "Self-Hosted" Mean?

A self-hosted trading platform runs on **your** computer — your Windows PC, Linux machine, or VPS. You install it, you configure it, you control it. All your markets, all your strategies, all your data — under your control.

```text
Cloud-hosted:
Your browser → Someone else's server → Exchange

Self-hosted (WolfBot Community):
Your machine → Exchange (directly)
```

---

## Why Self-Host?

### 1. Full Control
- Your machine, your rules
- No third party can pause your trading
- You decide when to update
- No service can be discontinued on you

### 2. Privacy
- API keys stay on your machine
- Trading data stays local
- No cloud database storing your strategies
- No third party sees your positions — across any market

### 3. Zero Cost
- WolfBot Community is completely free
- No subscription fees
- No per-trade fees
- Only cost is your own electricity/internet
- One platform covering all your markets — crypto, futures, and MT5

### 4. Security
- Non-custodial: no third party holds your funds
- Trade-only API keys: withdrawals are impossible
- Your firewall, your security rules
- No shared infrastructure attack surface

### 5. Customizability
- Full access to all features
- Run custom strategies across all connected markets
- Integrate with your own tools
- Modify to your workflow

## Common Concerns

### "Do I need a server?"
No. WolfBot Community runs fine on a regular Windows or Linux PC. A [VPS is only needed if you want 24/7 trading](/docs/run-24-7-on-a-vps) without keeping your PC on. It handles crypto exchanges and MT5 brokers equally well from any environment.

### "What if my PC crashes?"
Your positions are on the exchange, not your PC. WolfBot Community is non-custodial. When you restart, it recovers your positions automatically — whether they're crypto pairs or MT5 instruments.

### "Is it secure?"
Yes — more secure than cloud solutions in many ways. Your API keys never leave your machine. You control network access. Trade-only API keys prevent withdrawals even if someone gains access.

### "Can I still use it if I'm not technical?"
Yes. The Windows installer is a standard setup wizard — double-click, install, open. Linux uses a standard .deb package. No coding or Docker experience required.

## Is Self-Hosting Right for You?

### ✅ Self-hosting is good if you:
- Want to keep costs at zero
- Prefer controlling your own infrastructure
- Value privacy and data ownership
- Have a computer that can stay on (or a VPS)
- Are comfortable with basic computer setup

### ❌ Cloud might be better if you:
- Don't want to manage any infrastructure
- Need guaranteed 24/7 uptime
- Prefer direct support
- Want a fully managed experience
