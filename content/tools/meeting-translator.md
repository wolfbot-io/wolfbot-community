# Realtime Translate for Teams, Google Meet & Zoom — Break the Language Barrier

**Understand the meeting while it is happening — in your own language.**

Your team, client or partner speaks another language, and the meeting will not wait. WolfBot Community's Live Translate turns the audio of a meeting into live translation for you: read it as it appears, and hear it spoken back in a natural voice. It is **free**, it runs **on your own machine**, and it does not need the meeting platform to support it.

## Works with the meeting tools you already use

- **Microsoft Teams** (web)
- **Google Meet**
- **Zoom** (web client)
- **Any other browser-based call** — the platform does not matter

There is no bot that joins your call, no per-app extension to install, and nothing the meeting host has to enable. **If the audio plays in your browser tab, it can be translated.**

## Start in about a minute

1. Join the meeting in **Chrome or Edge**, using the web version of Teams, Meet or Zoom.
2. Open **Live Translate** and choose your target language (52 available).
3. Set the input mode to **Tab / System Audio**, pick the meeting tab in the browser's share picker and tick **"Share tab audio"**.
4. Follow the live translation on screen — and listen to it spoken back if your language has a voice (43 do).

Want to see your own words in the other language? Use the microphone mode of Live Translate to translate what you say — handy for preparing or checking what you want to tell them.

## Respect and privacy

Translating is not recording. Raw audio is never stored, transcript history is off unless you turn it on, and an Incognito mode keeps a session out of the local translation memory entirely. Still, be considerate: tell other participants when you use a translation aid, and follow your organisation's rules and local laws about meetings.

## Honest limits

- **Browser required.** Tab-audio capture needs Chrome or Edge on the computer running the translator. Desktop apps that do not run in a browser tab are not covered.
- **Linux today.** Realtime speech translation runs on Linux; Windows text and document translation is on the roadmap. A GPU makes it noticeably faster and more accurate.
- **Speed and accuracy depend on your hardware, the audio quality and the language pair.** Names, jargon and crosstalk can trip any speech system. Do not rely on it alone for legal, medical or financial decisions.
- Speech quality is most thoroughly tested on the first 24 languages; the other 28 are fully supported and still being tuned.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is realtime meeting translation really free?**
Yes. WolfBot Community is free and self-hosted, with no account, subscription or per-minute fee.

**Does a bot join my meeting?**
No. It listens to the audio of your browser tab, on your machine.

**Do the other participants need to install anything?**
No. Only you run Live Translate.

**Does it work with Zoom and Teams desktop apps?**
Not yet — use the web versions in Chrome or Edge.

**Is my meeting audio uploaded?**
No. Speech recognition and translation run locally.

**Which languages?**
52. English and Vietnamese are the most mature; the rest are usable at "beta" quality.
