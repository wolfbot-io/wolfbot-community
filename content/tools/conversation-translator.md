# Free Two-Way Conversation Translator — Talk Across Any Language

**Two people. Two languages. One screen. No language barrier.**

A traveller at a hotel desk, a support agent with an overseas customer, a business call with a partner, a family member who speaks another language: real conversations need both sides to be understood at the same time. WolfBot Community's two-way mode is a **free**, **private** realtime conversation translator that runs on your own machine.

It is part of [Live Translate](/tools/live-translate), the free realtime translator built into WolfBot Community. You do not need to be a trader to use it.

## How it works

The screen is split in two. **Each person types in their own language and reads the other person's message translated into theirs**, live. No copy-pasting between tabs, no picking languages every time.

1. Open **Live Translate** and choose **Two-way** — or go directly to `http://127.0.0.1:8080/translate/two-way` on the machine where WolfBot Community runs.
2. Pick the language for each side (52 available).
3. Start typing. Each side sees the other's words in their own language as the conversation goes.

Sit side by side or hand the keyboard across — it is designed for a real, in-person or screen-share conversation.

## Where it helps

- **Travel** — hotels, taxis, clinics, shops.
- **Customer support** — answer customers in their language without hiring a translator for every tongue.
- **Business calls** — keep a sensible written record of what each side said.
- **Family and friends** — the conversation that finally does not need a "middle person".

## Good to know

- Two-way mode is a typed conversation. For live spoken audio use Live Translate's speech mode.
- Translation quality is very good on common language pairs and still improving on "beta" languages. For legal, medical or financial matters, have a qualified person confirm anything important.
- Incognito mode keeps a session out of the local translation memory.
- Windows text translation is on the roadmap; the full realtime experience is on Linux today.

**Is it free?**
Yes. WolfBot Community is free and self-hosted, with no account or subscription.

**Does my conversation leave my computer?**
No. Translation runs locally.

**Do both people need WolfBot installed?**
No. It is one screen shared by both people.

**Can it translate speech?**
Two-way mode is typed. Spoken audio is handled by Live Translate's realtime speech mode.

**How many languages?**
52, chosen separately for each side.
