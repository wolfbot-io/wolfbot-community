# Live Translate — Break the Language Barrier with Local AI

**Understand anyone. Read anything. In your language.**

Live Translate is a free, local AI translation tool built directly into WolfBot Community. It translates speech and text in real time — across 52 languages — entirely on your own machine. No cloud account, no subscription, no audio or text ever leaves your computer.

It's also platform-agnostic: the same tool follows you into Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Zoom, YouTube, livestreams, X and any other browser-based call or stream — one translator, every platform, live.

You do not need to be a trader to use it. Live Translate is a genuinely useful, standalone AI tool that happens to live inside WolfBot Community — the same free, self-hosted platform that also runs your crypto, futures and MT5 trading when you're ready for that.

## Why "break the language barrier" matters

Most translation tools today are cloud services: you send your voice or your document to someone else's server, wait for a response, and trust that your data is handled responsibly. That is a real barrier of its own — for privacy, for cost, and for anyone who wants a tool that keeps working without a live internet dependency on a third party.

Live Translate takes a different approach:

> **Break language barriers — locally, privately, and in real time.**

The AI models run on your own hardware. Your conversations, your documents and your voice stay on your machine. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to send anywhere.

## What Live Translate actually does

| Capability | Status |
|---|---|
| Text translation | 52 languages |
| Speech recognition (understand spoken audio) | 52 languages |
| Natural voice output (hear the translation spoken back) | 43 languages |
| Runs fully offline of the cloud | ✅ Always |
| Account or subscription required | ❌ Never |

Three ways to use it today:

1. **Live speech translation** — speak into your microphone, or capture the audio from any browser tab — a video call, a livestream, a video — and see and hear the translation as it happens.
2. **Text translation** — type or paste any text and get an instant, streaming translation.
3. **Two-way conversation mode** — a split-screen view for two people speaking different languages, each side typing and reading in their own language. Useful for travel, support calls or any real conversation across a language gap.

## One translator for every meeting, call and stream

Most translation tools are built for one specific use case — a chat app, a document, a single meeting platform. Live Translate takes a platform-agnostic approach instead: it works with whatever audio is already playing in your browser.

Using your browser's own native "share audio" capability — no plugin, no per-app integration to install or maintain — Live Translate can listen to and translate, live:

- **Video calls** — Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Zoom Web Client, or any other browser-based meeting
- **YouTube** — videos, livestreams, and premieres in any language
- **Livestreams** — Twitch, YouTube Live and other browser-based streams
- **X (Twitter)** — video posts and Spaces played in your browser
- **Literally any website** that plays audio

If it plays sound in your browser, WolfBot can translate it in real time, spoken back to you or shown as live captions — no separate setup per platform, no waiting for that specific app to add a translation feature of its own.

## Real numbers, not marketing numbers

WolfBot Community would rather under-promise than over-claim. The table above reflects what is actually wired and certified in the current release, not a theoretical maximum:

- **Text translation** runs on TranslateGemma, a model family officially rated for 55 production-tier languages — WolfBot Community currently certifies **52** of them, in three rollout waves.
- **Speech recognition** runs on OpenAI's Whisper, wired for the same 52 certified languages.
- **Natural voice output** runs on Kokoro and Piper, which together currently cover **43** of those 52 languages with a natural-sounding voice. The other languages still translate perfectly in text — they simply do not have a voice yet.
- **English** and **Vietnamese** are WolfBot's most mature, "stable"-tier languages (Live Translate started life as an EN↔VI tool before going universal). Every other certified language is "beta" tier — fully usable today, still being refined.

## Why it's private by design

- Speech recognition, translation and voice synthesis all run locally, using a local Ollama model on your own machine.
- Nothing is logged by default: content logging is off, raw audio is never persisted, and transcript history is opt-in only.
- An Incognito mode exists for sessions you don't want remembered at all — no history row, no local cache entry.
- The only thing that ever leaves your machine, if you choose to enable it, is an anonymous usage count (which feature you used) — never the words you translated.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does this require an internet connection?**
No. Once the local AI model is downloaded during install, translation runs entirely offline of the cloud, on your own machine.

**Do I need a WolfBot Cloud account to use it?**
No. Live Translate is a Community-only feature — no account, no subscription. It is not available on WolfBot Cloud.

**Is this only for traders?**
No. It is a general-purpose translator. Trading is a separate, optional part of WolfBot Community — you can use Live Translate and never connect a broker at all.

**Which languages have the best quality right now?**
English and Vietnamese are the most mature ("stable" tier). Every other listed language is fully usable today at "beta" quality and continues to improve with each release.

**Does it work on Windows?**
Not yet for real-time speech — that is Linux-only today. A lighter Windows build (text, clipboard and document translation) is on the roadmap.
