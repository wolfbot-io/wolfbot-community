# Realtime Translate for YouTube, TikTok & Livestreams — Break the Language Barrier

**The stream is in another language. You do not have to be.**

The most interesting live content is rarely in your language: a foreign-language YouTube live, a TikTok live, a Twitch stream, an X video or Space, a market briefing, a conference talk. WolfBot Community's Live Translate translates whatever audio plays in your browser, **as it plays** — read it live and hear it spoken back. It is **free**, runs **on your own machine**, and does not depend on the platform at all.

## Works with any livestream or video

- **YouTube** — live streams, premieres and regular videos
- **TikTok** (web) — live and videos
- **Twitch**, **X (Twitter)** and other browser-based streams
- **Any site that plays audio** — webinars, conference streams, news, online classes

No subtitles need to exist. No creator or platform has to add a feature. **If there is audio, it can be translated.**

## Start in about a minute

1. Open the stream or video in **Chrome or Edge**.
2. Open **Live Translate** and pick your target language (52 available).
3. Set the input mode to **Tab / System Audio**, choose the stream's tab in the browser's share picker and tick **"Share tab audio"**.
4. Watch the stream while the live translation appears, and listen to it spoken back if your language has a voice (43 do).

## Honest limits

- **Browser required.** Tab-audio capture needs Chrome or Edge on the computer running the translator. Native mobile apps are not covered.
- **Linux today.** Realtime speech translation runs on Linux; Windows text and document translation is on the roadmap. A GPU makes it noticeably faster and more accurate.
- **Some protected streams may not share their audio.** If you hear no translation, check that "Share tab audio" was ticked.
- **Accuracy varies** with audio quality, music, background noise, accents, slang and the language pair. Speech quality is most thoroughly tested on the first 24 languages; the other 28 are fully supported and still being tuned.
- Respect the creator's terms and copyright: this translates for your own understanding and does not record or republish the stream.

Yes — use the microphone mode of Live Translate.
