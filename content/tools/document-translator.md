# Free Document & Subtitle Translator — Private, Local, 52 Languages

**Translate the whole file, not just a paragraph — without uploading it anywhere.**

Contracts, reports, slide decks, product manuals, exchange documentation, video subtitles: the files that matter most are often the ones you least want to upload to a stranger's server. WolfBot Community's document translator is **free**, runs **on your own machine**, and keeps your file's structure while it translates.

It is part of [Live Translate](/tools/live-translate), the free realtime translator built into WolfBot Community. You do not need to be a trader to use it.

## Supported formats

| Format | What you get |
|---|---|
| **DOCX** (Word) | Translated in place; headings, tables and structure are kept |
| **PPTX** (PowerPoint) | Translated in place; slides and speaker notes are kept |
| **PDF** | **Text-based PDFs only** — the text is translated. Scanned PDFs (images of text) are not supported yet |
| **SRT / VTT** | Subtitle files: numbering and timing are preserved; bilingual export supported |
| **TXT / Markdown / HTML** | Structure preserved (headings, lists, code blocks) |

## How it works

1. Open **Live Translate** in your dashboard and go to **Documents** — or open `http://127.0.0.1:8080/translate/documents` on the machine where WolfBot Community runs.
2. Add your file, choose the target language (leave the source on *Auto*).
3. Watch it translate **progressively** — the first blocks appear while the rest are still working, and large jobs can be cancelled and resumed.
4. Download the translated file (or a **bilingual** version with the original and the translation together — handy for subtitles and for checking the result).

## Built to protect your content

- **Nothing is uploaded.** The file and its translation stay on your machine.
- **URLs, code and numbers are protected** so they are not "translated" by mistake.
- **Glossary presets** (legal, finance, crypto, technology and more) keep terminology consistent across a long document, and brand names like WolfBot, TradingView and MT5 are kept verbatim.
- **Translation memory** makes repeated sentences instant and consistent.

## Free, and not tied to any platform

There is no per-page fee, no monthly plan, no file-size sales pitch and no account. It works on any file you have — regardless of who created it or which app it came from. The same install also gives you realtime speech translation for any audio in your browser — see [Live Translate](/tools/live-translate) — and realtime [text translation](/tools/text-translator).

## Honest limits

- **PDF is text-only.** Scanned pages and images with text need OCR, which is not available yet.
- **Complex formatting inside a single paragraph** (for example many different fonts or colours within one sentence) may be simplified in DOCX/PPTX output. Structure such as headings, tables and notes is preserved. Always review important files.
- Translation quality is very good on common language pairs and still improving on "beta" languages. For legal, medical or financial documents, have a qualified person check the result.
- Windows text and document translation is on the roadmap; the full experience is on Linux today.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it really free?**
Yes. WolfBot Community is free and self-hosted; there is no account, subscription or page limit.

**Is my document uploaded anywhere?**
No. Translation runs locally on your machine.

**Can it translate scanned PDFs?**
Not yet. Text-based PDFs work; scanned images need OCR.

**Can I keep the original next to the translation?**
Yes — export a bilingual version.

**Which languages?**
52 languages. English and Vietnamese are the most mature; the rest are usable at "beta" quality.
