# Free Realtime Text Translator — Local, Private, 52 Languages

**Type in your language. Read it in theirs — as you type.**

Text translation is the fastest way to break a language barrier: an email from an overseas partner, a support chat, an exchange announcement, a message you need to send. Most tools send every word to someone else's server. WolfBot Community's text translator is different: it is **free**, it runs **on your own machine**, and it translates in **real time** while you type.

It is part of [Live Translate](/tools/live-translate), the free realtime translator built into WolfBot Community. You do not need to be a trader to use it.

## Three ways to translate text

1. **Quick Translate** — paste or type text, pick the target language, and read the translation as it streams in. Leave the source on *Auto* and the language is detected for you.
2. **Live Typing** — translate while you type. Only the part you changed is re-translated, and an out-of-date result is cancelled the moment you type more, so the translation never lags behind and never overwrites your newer text.
3. **Two-way conversation** — two people, two languages, one screen. See [Conversation Translator](/tools/conversation-translator).

Open it from **Live Translate** in the dashboard sidebar, or directly at `http://127.0.0.1:8080/translate/text` on the machine where WolfBot Community runs.

## What makes it useful day to day

- **Fast on ordinary hardware.** In our own test on a desktop with an RTX 3060, short sentences translated in about half a second once the model was warm (the first request after start-up takes a few seconds). Speed depends on your hardware.
- **Glossary presets.** Pick a domain — general, technology, engineering, medical, legal, finance, crypto, education or travel — and domain terms are translated consistently. Brand and product names such as WolfBot, TradingView and MT5 are kept exactly as written.
- **Translation memory.** Text you have already translated is remembered locally, so repeats are instant and consistent.
- **Numbers, links and code stay intact.** URLs, code and numbers are protected instead of being "translated".
- **Incognito mode.** Turn it on and nothing from that session is written to the local translation memory.

## Good to know

- Translation quality is very good on common language pairs and still improving on the "beta" languages. For legal, medical or financial decisions, have a qualified person check the result.
- Text translation needs a local translation model (installed with WolfBot Community). A computer with a GPU is noticeably faster, but it is not required.
- Windows text and document translation is on the roadmap; the full realtime experience is on Linux today.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the text translator really free?**
Yes. WolfBot Community is free and self-hosted, and there is no account, subscription or usage limit tied to translation.

**Does my text leave my computer?**
No. Translation runs locally on your machine.

**Which languages are supported?**
52 languages for text. English and Vietnamese are the most mature; every other language is fully usable at "beta" quality.

**Does it need an internet connection?**
Not for translating. Once the local model is installed, translation does not depend on a cloud service.

**Can it translate as I type?**
Yes — that is Live Typing.
