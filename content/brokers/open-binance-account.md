# How to Open a Binance Account with WolfBot

This guide takes you from no account to one ready for a separate, trade-only WolfBot Community API key. You do not need to deposit funds or enable withdrawals to complete it.

> **Referral disclosure:** the button attributes WolfBot as the referrer. Binance alone determines any reward, eligibility rule, or fee benefit; WolfBot does not promise one.

**[Open the WolfBot Binance registration page →](https://www.binance.com/register?ref=WOLFBOT)**

![Four-step Binance account-opening flow: WolfBot referral, registration, identity verification, and authenticator 2FA](/images/guides/open-binance-account.png)

*This is an instructional diagram, not a Binance UI screenshot. Menu names vary by app version and jurisdiction.*

## Before you start

- Use a long-term personal email already protected with 2FA.
- Create a unique password; never reuse your email, WolfBot, or another exchange password.
- Prepare a password manager, authenticator app, camera phone, and eligible unexpired identity document.
- Use truthful residency information and a stable network; do not use a VPN to misrepresent your region.

## Step 1: verify the destination and referral

1. Open the WolfBot link above in a new tab.
2. Confirm HTTPS and a hostname ending in `binance.com` before typing anything.
3. Select your actual country/region. Stop if Binance says service is unavailable there.
4. If shown, confirm the referral field contains `WOLFBOT`.

## Step 2: create your login

1. Choose **Email** or **Phone** registration; email is often easier for alerts and recovery.
2. Enter the address/number you control and request the verification code.
3. Enter it only on the Binance page. Never relay an OTP to a person claiming to be support.
4. Save a unique password in your password manager; do not screenshot it.
5. Sign out and sign in once to confirm the credentials work.

## Step 3: complete identity verification

1. Open the profile menu and find **Identification**, **Verification**, or **Get Verified**.
2. Choose an individual/personal account when registering for yourself.
3. Select the actual document-issuing country and document type.
4. Enter your legal name, date of birth, and address exactly as required.
5. Photograph the original document with sharp text, even light, no glare or edits, and all corners visible.
6. Complete the selfie/liveness check in good light without covering your face.
7. Review status in Binance. If rejected, correct the stated issue rather than reusing the same image.

## Step 4: secure the account before funding it

1. Go to **Profile → Security** and enable authenticator-app 2FA.
2. Scan the QR code/enter the setup key and confirm with the rotating code.
3. Store the recovery key offline and separately from your password.
4. Enable anti-phishing code, login alerts, and passkey when available.
5. Review devices/account activity and remove unknown sessions.
6. Test a complete sign-out/sign-in cycle with 2FA before depositing.

## Ready-for-WolfBot checklist

- [ ] Domain and referral checked.
- [ ] Contact verification and required KYC complete.
- [ ] Authenticator 2FA works and recovery is stored safely.
- [ ] Nobody else has your password, OTP, or 2FA device.
- [ ] You read the [trade-only API key guide](/brokers/api-key-guide).

Next, follow [Connect Binance to WolfBot](/brokers/binance). Create a new key labelled `WolfBot Community`, enable only required read/trade permissions, and keep **Withdrawals and Transfer disabled**. Start with [Simulation](/docs/simulation) and configure [Risk Controls](/docs/risk-controls) before Live.

## Common problems

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Verification email missing | Check spam, spelling, and mailbox capacity; respect the resend timer. |
| Email/phone already exists | Use password recovery instead of creating a duplicate. |
| KYC rejected | Retake the unexpired original with all corners visible and no glare. |
| Region unsupported | Stop; WolfBot cannot bypass Binance regional restrictions. |
| Authenticator lost | Use saved recovery or Binance's official recovery flow, never an unofficial unlock service. |
